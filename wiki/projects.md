## NanoVisionAI — AI Microscopy Startup

- **What**: An LLM-based tool enhancing microscopy resolution 3–7x in a sample-independent manner; research published in *Nature Communications* (source: Amilia_Liu_Resume.docx).
- **Role**: Co-Founder & CEO — product strategy, fundraising, partnerships.
- **Methods**: LLM/AI model development; commercialization across 15 academic and industry partners.
- **Outcome**: Pitched to 100+ investors in 6 months; sourced clients for expansion.
- **Status**: Active (Aug 2024 – present). See [[work-experience]] and [[impact-metrics]].

## Google — Multilingual Search Reranking Pipeline (`search-quality-reranker`)

- **What**: A reranking layer re-scoring retrieval results with fine-tuned PaLM 2 embeddings to fix poor NDCG for low-resource languages (source: Google_SWE_Internship.md).
- **Role**: SWE intern; primary builder.
- **Methods**: Cross-lingual transfer learning; PaLM 2, TensorFlow.
- **Outcome**: +12% NDCG@10 across 8 languages (+18% Swahili).
- **Status**: Approved for production deployment Q4 2026.

## Google — Automated Ranking-Quality Eval Framework
